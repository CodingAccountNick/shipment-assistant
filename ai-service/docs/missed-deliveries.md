# Missed deliveries

## Nobody at the address
If the recipient is not at the address, the driver leaves a card and tries again the next working day.

## After two failed attempts
After two failed delivery attempts, the parcel goes to the nearest pickup point. It is kept there for 7 days, and the recipient gets an email with the pickup point's address and opening hours.

## Returning to the sender
Parcels that are not collected from the pickup point within 7 days are returned to the sender. The sender pays the return delivery cost.
