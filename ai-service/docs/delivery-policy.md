# Delivery policy

## Delivery times
Standard deliveries within the EU arrive in 2 to 4 working days. Express deliveries arrive the next working day if they are booked before 2pm.

## Delivery hours
Drivers deliver between 8am and 6pm, Monday to Saturday. There are no deliveries on Sundays or public holidays.

## Signatures
Parcels worth more than 500 euros need a signature on delivery. Other parcels can be left in a safe place if the recipient has agreed to it in advance.
