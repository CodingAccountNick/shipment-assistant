# Delays

## Common causes
Shipments can be delayed by bad weather, customs checks, road closures, or high volumes during holidays.

## What happens when a shipment is delayed
When a shipment is delayed, its status changes to Delayed and the recipient gets an email with the new estimated delivery date.

## Compensation
If an express delivery is more than 5 working days late, the sender can claim a refund of the delivery fee.
